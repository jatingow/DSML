# Module 2 – Applied Statistics for Data Science

Welcome to **Module 2**!

This module covers core statistical principles and practical techniques required for exploratory analysis, experimental design, and machine learning intuition.

---

## 📚 Contents

### 💻 Practical Notebooks

1. **[`Descriptive_Statistics_Hands_On_Colab.ipynb`](file:///d:/DSML/ML-DataScience-Training/Module2/Descriptive_Statistics_Hands_On_Colab.ipynb)**
   - **Focus**: Descriptive statistics, summary metrics, and business interpretation.
   - **Key Concepts**:
     - Measures of central tendency (Mean, Median, Mode).
     - Measures of dispersion (Range, Variance, Standard Deviation).
     - Percentiles, Quartiles, and Interquartile Range (IQR).
     - Outlier detection and business context interpretation.

2. **[`Sampling_and_Central_Limit_Theorem.ipynb`](file:///d:/DSML/ML-DataScience-Training/Module2/Sampling_and_Central_Limit_Theorem.ipynb)**
   - **Focus**: Inferential statistics and probability theory.
   - **Key Concepts**:
     - Population vs. sample characteristics.
     - Sampling distributions and sample size effects.
     - Central Limit Theorem (CLT) simulation using 1,000 sample means.

3. **[`Statistics_Mini_project_salesData.ipynb`](file:///d:/DSML/ML-DataScience-Training/Module2/Statistics_Mini_project_salesData.ipynb)**
   - **Focus**: Applied statistical testing on real-world sales data.
   - **Key Concepts**:
     - Parametric hypothesis testing and $p$-value evaluation.
     - Distribution fitting using `scipy.stats`.
     - A/B testing simulation and correlation heatmaps.
